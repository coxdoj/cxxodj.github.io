Privacy Policy

The Third Floor is designed so your private practice stays on your iPhone.

Information the app collects

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data. It does not contain advertising or analytics services.

Information stored on your device

Your writing, progress, saved items, continuation history, events, and preferences are stored locally on your device. The Third Floor does not send this material to us or automatically share it.

Your control

You can clear private writing or erase all Third Floor data from Privacy & Settings inside the app. Removing the app may also remove locally stored app data, subject to your device and backup settings.

Network access and external links

The reflective, reading, and writing experience works without a network service. If you choose a legal or support link, iOS opens this website in your browser. That visit is separate from your private app content.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this policy and may process standard connection information when serving it. We do not add analytics or advertising trackers to these Third Floor pages.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. A parent or guardian should decide whether the reflective content is suitable for a child.

Changes and contact

If data practices change, this page will show a new effective date. Privacy questions can be sent through Third Floor Support.
